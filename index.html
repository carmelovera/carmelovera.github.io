Hi, I'm Carmelo

I'm a software developer based in Seattle, originally from Argentina. I recently earned my Bachelor of Applied Science in Software Development.

For nearly four years I worked on websites in higher education, including two years leading a web team that managed WordPress websites for multiple departments from first request to launch. I worked with each department to gather requirements, coordinated tasks and deadlines, and reviewed every site before it went live. Along the way, I restructured navigation so students could reach information in fewer clicks, simplified content, tested sites for ADA accessibility, and improved their SEO.

Right now I'm building a Time Tracker app, a simple way to track the time you put into the things that help you grow. Personally, I use it for my job search, the gym and personal development, so I can see the real progress I'm making, not just the feeling of being busy.
